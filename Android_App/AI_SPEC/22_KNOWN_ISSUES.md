# Known Issues — LeslieCleaner Android 3.0.0

- KI-3-001: inventory and boot-receiver visibility are provider/package-visibility limited.
- KI-3-002: Android does not expose one universal public autostart switch; OEM settings remain authoritative.
- KI-3-003: LeslieCleaner does not replace Google Play Protect and cannot establish a malware verdict from public APIs.
- KI-3-004: only app-private cache is measured directly; other-app cleanup requires system/OEM confirmation.
- KI-3-005: live cloud rule delivery is not configured until endpoint, signing and privacy decisions are approved.
